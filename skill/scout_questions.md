# Scout questions (Direction step)

Opponent DEFENSE questions, grouped by position like Alabama's "Game Plan Opponent Questions" sheet (Coach shared it 10/21/2026)
and cut to what matters for our offense. Copy this file into the week folder as `scout/scout_questions_<opp>.md` at intake,
add or cut questions with Coach, then collect and report ONLY what answers them. Every report section names the question it
answers; a section that answers none gets cut before Coach sees it.

**Who answers each question**
- **[R] Readers can count it** from pre-snap frames (end zone + wide) or Hudl Assist. Goes in the report as numbers.
- **[R→C] Readers flag, Coach confirms.** Needs the 1-2 s after the snap. Readers pick candidate snaps; Coach makes the call
  on a board. Never printed until Coach confirms (readers were 0 of 6 on pressure and 8 of 14 on coverage at OPJ).
- **[C] Coach's eye.** Judgment: who's good, how aggressive, technique. Claude builds a short clip list per question
  (the 8-12 snaps that show it); Coach watches only those and writes the answer.
- **[O] Off film.** Coaching staff, injuries, what other coaches say. Coach or the staff.

## DL
1. [R] How does the DL align: L/R, field/boundary, to the Y, to the RB? Where is the 3 technique and the shade?
   (Test every key on the snaps where they disagree; see the rules below.)
2. [R] What fronts do they play vs each of our formations? Any ID issues (3-down with 4-down personnel, standing edge,
   TITE, BEAR)?
3. [R→C] Do they stem or move the DL before the snap? From what to what?
4. [R→C] Do they slant or angle at the snap? Which way, and what is the tell?
5. [C] Best run defender, best pass rusher, worst DL. Do we need a plan for him, or should we run at him?
6. [C] Can we reach their ends? Is there one we want to run at?

## LBs and overhangs
7. [R] How do the LBs align: depth, width, field/boundary, strength? Is there a designated walk / apex player to the field
   or the slot?
8. [R] Vs 2x2, does the overhang walk out to the slot or cheat to the box?
9. [R→C] Do the LBs mug the A gaps or creep, and do they come (green dog / pressure) or bail?
10. [C] How do the LBs fit our runs (fast flow, scrape, spill or box)? Do our OL have to come off combos quickly?
11. [C] Best and worst LB. Any LB a mismatch for our RB in protection?

## Secondary
12. [R] Shell before the snap by formation and down & distance; safety depth and width.
13. [R] Corner alignment: depth, leverage, when do they press?
14. [R→C] Rotation at the snap (sky, buzz, cloud)? To the field, to the trips, to the boundary?
15. [R→C] Who forces the run, and how fast do the safeties fill? (The best tell for the called coverage on run plays.)
16. [C] Coverage by formation and situation (Cover 4 / 2 / 3 / 1 / man). Coach tags it; readers only give the shell.
17. [C] Best and worst DB. How aggressive are the corners in press and on quick game? Eyes on the receiver or the QB?

## Pressure
18. [R→C] Do they blitz or sim? When (down & distance, field zone), from where, and what coverage is behind it?
19. [R→C] What is the tell (walk-up, depth creep, safety roll, late movement)?

## Formation and motion adjustments
20. [R] What happens when our formation is into the boundary?
21. [R] How do they adjust to bunch (TRIBE), nub (TROOP), EMPTY (aligned and motioned to), and our motions (ORBIT, FLY, ZIP, PUSH)?
22. [R] Do they sub by our personnel or by down & distance?

## Situations
23. [R] What changes by down & distance, field zone (backed up own 1-10 / coming out own 11-19 / open own 20 to +40 /
    fringe +39 to +21 / high red +20 to +11 / low red +10 to +5 / goal line +4 to goal), and hash?
24. [R] Explosives and TDs allowed: formation, play, front, shell (facts only; Coach adds the why).

## Off film (Coach / staff)
25. [O] Who is the DC, what is his background, and is the HC involved with the defense?
26. [O] Injuries and starters (theirs and ours).
27. [O] What did coaches who played them say?
28. [O] Staff: our advantages, what could wreck our plan, how we win.

## Rules for answering
- **Test every tendency against the other keys.** When a split shows up (e.g. "the 3 goes away from the Y"), list every key that
  could explain it (Y side, RB side, hash/boundary, receiver strength, personnel) and check the snaps where the keys DISAGREE.
  The key that wins those snaps is the rule. (OPJ 10/20: "UNDER, 3 away from the Y" was really "3 to the boundary":
  14 of 16 when the Y was in the boundary. The player also moved with the hash: #88 left on the left hash, right on the right.)
- **[R→C] and [C] answers are Coach's.** Readers' post-snap calls never print on their own (memory report-facts-from-coach-calls).
- **End with 3-4 rules** the booth can use (one line each, with the count), plus the first-series check that confirms each one.
